## 5. 表格总脚注：投票权口径与 49.9% 上限机制（L17746–17760）

- **【F29】脚注 "*"**（L17747–17748）："Represents beneficial ownership of less than 1%."（代表低于 1% 的实益持股）。
- **【F30】脚注 "†"——总投票权百分比的分母口径**（L17749–17754 原文）：
  > "Represents the percentage of the total votes attributable to all outstanding shares of our Class A common stock and Class F common stock. Does not include (i) our Class T common stock, which is held by the LTBT, and which generally has the authority to elect and remove the Class T directors, who together will constitute a majority of the authorized members of our board of directors, and is generally not entitled to vote with respect to matters submitted to a vote of stockholders, (ii) our Class N common stock, which is not entitled to vote on any matter that is submitted to a vote of stockholders, except as otherwise required by law, or (iii) our Class E common stock, which is not entitled to vote for the election of directors."

  要点：总投票权 % 仅以 **Class A + Class F** 两类发行在外股份所附表决票为分母；**不计入** (i) LTBT 持有的 Class T（一般有权选举/罢免 Class T 董事，而该等董事**合计构成董事会授权成员的多数**；对提交股东表决的事项一般无投票权）、(ii) Class N（除法律另有要求外对任何股东表决事项均无投票权）、(iii) Class E（无权在董事选举中投票）。
- **【F31】各类股投票权设置**（L17755–17757 原文）：
  > "Holders of our Class A common stock are entitled to one vote per share on all matters submitted to stockholders generally. Our Class F common stock is entitled to a fixed 50.1% of the voting power of our outstanding capital stock as of the applicable record date on matters submitted to stockholders generally. As a result, the voting power of the holders of Class A common stock is limited to an aggregate of 49.9% of the total outstanding voting power as of the applicable record date."

  即：Class A 每股 1 票；**Class F 在提交股东表决的一般事项上固定享有全部发行在外股本 50.1% 的投票权**；因此 **Class A 全体持有人的投票权合计被限制在总投票权的 49.9%**。
- **【F32】49.9% 折算口径**（L17757–17759）："Accordingly, the voting power of each holder of Class A common stock has been reduced to 49.9% of the percentage shown before the offering (taking into account the reduction of voting power due to shares sold in the offering)."——表中每位 Class A 持有人的投票权百分比已按发行前比例乘以 49.9% 折算（并考虑发行中售出股份导致的投票权稀释）。
